What I calculate

  • ≈9.08bn remote vs 38.72bn non-remote card transactions.
  • ≈€59.46 remote vs €32.54 non-remote average ticket from rounded aggregates.
  • ≈1.83× remote/non-remote average-ticket ratio.
  • ≈68.8% of all card count represented by contactless transactions.
  • ≈20% increase in reported aggregate fraud value from 2023 to 2024.
The point is not the arithmetic. It is the discipline: source, denominator, derivation, limitation, and no merchant-level inference from market aggregates.
