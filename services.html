Services / initial commercial scope

Three things to buy.

The initial practice is intentionally narrow: diagnose payment loss, isolate authorization/routing failure, or maintain a continuous payment-performance decision desk. More specialized audit modules sit inside those engagements rather than becoming a menu of twenty products.

01 / DIAGNOSTIC

Checkout Leak Audit

Outcome: find where payment value is failing across authorization, authentication, declines, retries, capture/refund and reconciliation.

  • Data QA + metric dictionary
  • failed-value waterfall
  • decline/3DS/retry diagnostics
  • routing and payment-method analysis
  • 30-day action register

Decision classes: FIX / INVESTIGATE / TEST / DO NOT TOUCH.

02 / ACCEPTANCE

Authorization / Routing Audit

Outcome: isolate approval leakage without treating raw PSP differences as causal uplift.

  • issuer/BIN and country matrices
  • route/method/device mix adjustment
  • amount-band and time analysis
  • fraud/cost guardrails
  • randomized experiment design where feasible

Primary deliverable: prioritized intervention map with identification status.

03 / RETAINER

Payment Performance Desk

Outcome: continuous monitoring and decision support once a stable production data feed exists.

  • authorization deterioration
  • issuer/PSP shifts
  • 3DS and decline changes
  • retry and fraud movement
  • cost and settlement exceptions
  • experiment tracking + monthly memo

Status: production service available when client data perimeter and controls are agreed.
