02 / ACCEPTANCE

Authorization / Routing Audit

Outcome: isolate approval leakage without treating raw PSP differences as causal uplift.

Scoped after data fit-check
  • issuer/BIN and country matrices
  • route/method/device mix adjustment
  • amount-band and time analysis
  • fraud/cost guardrails
  • randomized experiment design where feasible

Primary deliverable: prioritized intervention map with identification status.

03 / RETAINER

Payment Performance Desk

Outcome: continuous monitoring and decision support once a stable production data feed exists.

Custom monthly scope
  • authorization deterioration
  • issuer/PSP shifts
  • 3DS and decline changes
  • retry and fraud movement
  • cost and settlement exceptions
  • experiment tracking + monthly memo

Status: available when client data perimeter and controls are agreed.

Pricing policy. The €1,500–€2,500 range is founding production pricing while the first two properly documented production records are built. Once two engagements have defensible production evidence, delivery records and permission-safe case studies, comparable audits move toward roughly €4,000–€8,000+ depending on transaction volume, processors, markets, data condition, implementation support and experiment scope. This is a commercial policy, not a claim about realized client value.
