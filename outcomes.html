Case-study standard

Every future production case must show the whole chain.

A case is useful only when a buyer can see what was measured, what was merely suspected, what changed, how the change was tested, and what remains uncertain.

BASELINE + ISSUE

Period, denominator, value at risk, segmentation, data quality and exact failure mode.

EVIDENCE + INTERVENTION

Observed association, causal status, chosen action, implementation record and guardrails.

EXPERIMENT + OUTCOME

Measurement window, comparator, effect estimate, realized state, limitations and publication permission.
