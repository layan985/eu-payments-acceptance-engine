Client outcomes / validation register

Outcomes are published at their strongest completed evidence state.

This page is the public-facing layer for Checkout engagement and outcome records. Commissioned work, production analysis, implementation, validation and realized value remain separate states rather than being collapsed into a single case-study label.

Outcome ladder

One commercial claim. Five validation states.

The operating rule is simple: never promote an estimated opportunity into a realized-value claim, and never call an observed difference causal without the design to support it.

01

LOSS OBSERVED

A measured failure or value concentration exists in the relevant data perimeter. Recoverability is not implied.

02

OPPORTUNITY ESTIMATED

A bounded model estimates what may be recoverable, with assumptions, uncertainty and exclusions disclosed.

03

EXPERIMENT VALIDATED

A pre-specified intervention test supports an incremental effect with fraud, cost and operational guardrails.

04

IMPLEMENTED

The intervention moved beyond diagnosis or test and was deployed in the relevant operating environment.

05

FINANCIALLY REALIZED

Value is traceable to accounting, settlement or another validated realized-value evidence surface.
