Payment Performance Lab is the commercial identity around the Checkout workstream: merchant payment diagnostics, experimentation, public payments research and payment-system forensics. Founder attribution belongs here, not inside every finding.

OPERATING MODEL

Evidence before scale.

The practice does not claim employees, institutional clients, certifications, partnerships or realized client outcomes that are not documented. External specialists can be added for bounded review or engagement work with their role and validation scope recorded.

Governance

Procurement-relevant rules are public.

The governance layer is designed so confidentiality, evidence, outcome measurement and methodological corrections are inspectable rather than implied.

Canonical evidence classes, causal-language thresholds, mandatory claim records and falsification rules.

Outcome Validation

Corrections

Material errors require status changes, version increments, correction notes and dependent reruns where practical.

Research Independence

Commercial engagement does not change evidence labels, suppress adverse guardrails or convert descriptive evidence into causal claims.

Provider Trademark Boundary

Provider names describe interoperability or test evidence. No partnership, endorsement or certification is implied without a current verifiable basis.

Security posture

Production payment data is minimized.

The default analytical perimeter excludes PAN, CVV/CVC, authentication secrets, private keys and unnecessary directly identifying customer data.

Access

MFA, least privilege, segregated client workspaces where practical, access logging for production datasets.

Storage & transfer

Encrypted transfer/storage, agreed retention period, deletion confirmation where required, no public raw client data.

Claims

No PCI DSS, scheme, processor or provider certification is claimed unless actually obtained and verifiable.
