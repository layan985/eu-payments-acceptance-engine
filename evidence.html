Evidence architecture

Every object stays in its real class.

The institutional layer is not a new proof claim. It is the index connecting evidence, case files, outcomes, methods, sources, QA, validation and limitations already produced across Checkout.

Official / public evidence

ECB/EBA context, processor disclosures, public first-party merchant architecture and regulatory evidence.

Controlled / experimental evidence

Synthetic merchant diagnostics, randomized synthetic experiments and reproducible analysis code.

Provider / operational evidence

Executed provider-test flows, authentication states, capture/refund lifecycle, webhook verification and payment-state controls.

Commercial evidence

Audit reports, client-facing rooms, decision memos, technical appendices, action registers and outcome records.

Validation evidence

Review, reproduction, implementation and outcome-validation objects are indexed independently rather than blended into the underlying case.

Maturity labels

Method demonstration · provider-test case · public-data research · commissioned audit · production audit · implementation completed · outcome validated · externally reviewed.
