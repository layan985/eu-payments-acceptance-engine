Evidence architecture

Every object stays in its real class.

Completed client engagements are real commercial proof. That does not automatically turn every client-facing number into public production evidence, every recommendation into an implemented outcome, or every observed change into causal uplift.

Client evidence

Four completed engagements. Client identity, raw data and outcome detail are exposed only where the relevant record permits disclosure.

Official / public evidence

ECB/EBA context, processor disclosures, public first-party merchant architecture and regulatory evidence.

Controlled / experimental evidence

Synthetic merchant diagnostics, randomized synthetic experiments and reproducible analysis code.

Provider / operational evidence

Executed provider-test flows, authentication states, capture/refund lifecycle, webhook verification and payment-state controls.

Validation evidence

Implementation, experiment validation, external review, reproduction and realized-value evidence are recorded separately from engagement completion.

Maturity labels

Commissioned · completed · production evidence · implemented · outcome validated · externally reviewed · independently reproduced are separate states.

Canonical evidence

Client work is no longer buried under demonstrations.

The institutional site now starts from the real commercial record: four completed engagements. Controlled and public evidence remain underneath as technical substantiation and methods proof.
