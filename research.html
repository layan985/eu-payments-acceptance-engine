Research

Payments research built to survive interrogation.

The research program is organized around merchant acceptance, authentication, economics, European payments, payment operations and experimentation. Public-source research and merchant outcomes remain separate evidence classes.

Authorization Science

Issuer behavior, routing, network tokens, soft declines, stored credentials, amount effects and cohort stability.

Authentication

3DS, SCA, exemptions, challenge friction, post-authentication issuer behavior and fraud trade-offs.

Merchant Economics

Processing cost, route economics, failed-value decomposition and net good-payment value.

European Payments

ECB/EBA evidence, instant payments, SEPA, VoP and regulatory/market structure.

Payment Operations

Reconciliation, webhooks, capture, refunds, settlement, payout and payment-state integrity.

Experimentation

Randomized payment tests, pre-specified decision rules, uncertainty and rollout guardrails.

Publication queue

Sequential, not decorative.

Planned publications remain planned until released with data, methods, provenance and versioning.
