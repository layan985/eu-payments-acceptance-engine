## How long does it take?

The standard engagement is designed as a **5-business-day audit** once the agreed input dataset is available and the measurement contract is locked. Material data-quality problems can pause analysis because Checkout will not hide a broken denominator behind a delivery deadline.

## What data do you need?

At minimum: stable payment attempt or intent IDs, timestamps, amount/currency, market, payment method, route/PSP where relevant and the result/state. Authentication, decline, retry, lifecycle, fraud/cost and reconciliation fields are added only where needed by the decision question.

See [Security & Confidentiality](/security) for the data perimeter.

## What if Checkout finds no economically defensible intervention?

**That is a valid result. The final decision may be `DO NOT TOUCH`.**

The audit is not paid to manufacture a recommendation. A raw processor difference can disappear after eligibility or mix adjustment; a retry idea can fail fraud/cost guardrails; a 3DS difference can be selection rather than friction. Avoiding an unjustified implementation is a legitimate decision outcome.

## Do you need PAN data?

No. Full PAN, CVV/CVC and card-track data are outside the default analytical perimeter. Checkout normally works with merchant-generated IDs, provider object IDs, pseudonymized references and non-sensitive payment-state fields.

## Can you work with aggregated data?

Sometimes. Aggregated data can be enough for a bounded diagnostic or measurement review, but it usually cannot support retry sequencing, event-state integrity, contamination checks or credible causal attribution. The audit will state what the available grain can and cannot establish.

## Do you replace our PSP or acquirer?

No. Checkout is an independent diagnostic and measurement engagement. It does not require replacing a provider and does not receive compensation for recommending one route or processor over another.

## Do you guarantee uplift?

No. Checkout guarantees a defined analytical process and deliverable scope, **not a positive uplift result**. Observed differences are not called causal, modeled opportunity is not called realized revenue, and an experiment can correctly conclude that no change should be made.

## Who owns the outputs?

The client receives the engagement deliverables and analytical outputs defined in the written scope. Any reusable pre-existing methodology, public research code or generic framework remains separately identifiable. Project-specific ownership and usage terms are set in the engagement agreement rather than implied on the website.

## Will our name or results be published?

Not by default. Public use requires explicit permission. Client identity, alias, volume, payment value, findings, metrics, testimonial and implementation status are treated as separate disclosure categories. A confidential engagement can remain confidential.

The founding fixed scope includes the measurement/data-QA layer, failed-value and authorization diagnostics, decline analysis, 3DS/retry analysis where data supports it, route/economic screening where identifiable, payment-state/reconciliation exceptions where relevant, experiment recommendations, a limitations register, a technical appendix and the executive decision register.
