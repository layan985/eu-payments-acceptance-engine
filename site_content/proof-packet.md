## Validation rule

Completed counts: **3 external reviews · 1 independent reproduction · 1 paid audit · 1 measured production case · 1 testimonial · 1 referral/repeat engagement.**

The evidence classes remain separate. External validation does not convert synthetic results into production results, and the production case does not make an observational processor gap causal.
